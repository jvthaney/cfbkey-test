Image Sources

Most images in the key were created by the UNH for Freshwater Biology authors. Dr. Richard S. Stemberger contributed numerous images of rotifers, as well as some cladocerans and copepods. Dr. Stemberger provided all ecological and additional information on rotifers unless otherwise noted. Dr. James Morin contributed cladoceran images from the Isles of Shoals, ME. Selected calanoid and cyclopoid copepod images were provided by the “Calanoid copepods of the Laurentian Great Lakes” by Lesko et al. (2003) and “Cyclopoid copepods of the Laurentian Great Lakes" by Hudson et al. (2003).
