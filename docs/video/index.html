Video clips

Short clips of living zooplankton, microscope views and sampling methods. The clips for each species also appear near the bottom of that species' page.

Types of video in this key

  1. Live videos of organisms. Videos were taken of organisms freshly collected in the field.
  2. Virtual Focus. Videos capturing the microscopic anatomy of an individual organism. These interactive videos allow you to discern key features by adjusting the depth of focus on a particular characteristic of the organism.
  3. Sampling methods. Videos covering a range of methods for collecting plankton. These videos demonstrate sampling techniques and include underwater views of how standard sampling devices operate in situ.
  4. Dissection demonstration. To assist with dissection of calanoid and cyclopoid copepods, we have included video clips of procedures as seen through a dissecting microscope.

Sampling methods

"Jerk" Closing Net -- Used for sampling plankton at depth intervals.

Bongo Net -- A pair of plankton nets towed together to obtian replicate samples or samples using different mesh sizes.

Field Processing of Plankton Samples -- Effective method for concentrating zooplankton samples.

Gravity Water Sampler -- A simple water sampler activated by tugging the line.

Grazing Chamber-- Designed to measure feeding rates of zooplankton.

Juday Trap -- A mechanical framed trap used to collect a fixed volume of water that passes through a plankton net (forerunner to Schindler Patalas trap).

Kemmerer Bottle -- An early and widely used water bottle for collecting water samples at depth.

Messenger Closing Net -- Used for sampling plankton at depth intervals.

Meyer Bottle Sampler -- One of the simplest and earliest methods used to obtain water samples at depth.

Peristaltic Pump -- Small volume sampling at specific depths.

Rigid Tube Sampler -- A simple device for shallow integrated sampling.

Van Dorn Water Bottle -- Used for collecting water at discrete depths.
