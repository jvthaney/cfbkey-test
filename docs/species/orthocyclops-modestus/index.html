Orthocyclops modestus

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Maxillopoda
Subclass
Copepoda
Order
Cyclopoida
Family
Cyclopidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Inner margin of caudal ramus hairy.
  • Distinct groove on caudal ramus beginning at lateral seta insertion.
  • Fifth leg has 3 distinct segments.
  • Antennule of 16 segments.
