Euchlanis pellucida

Phylum
Rotifera
Class
Monogononta
Order
Ploima
Family
Brachionidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Toes long and slender,slightly enlarged posteriorly, but pointed distally
  • Body rounded and triradiate in cross-sectional view
  • Lengths: dorsal plate 300-500 µm, toes 90-150 µm.
  • Widths: dorsal plate 270-450 µm
