Part of Cyclops scutifer

Cyclops scutifer Swimming Towards Daphnia pulex

  • Figure 2. Cyclops scutifer (right) swimming towards the Daphnia pulex (left) a possible prey item (5). This Schlieren optical image shows the hydrodynamic disturbances caused by both species. The C. scutifer exhibits the typical hop and sink pattern while the D. pulex causes less disturbance (5). Sensilla distributed on the zooplankters carapaces detect minute disturbances in the water helping to avoid predation, capture prey, and find mates (5). (Modified after Strickler and Bal, 1973.)
