Arctodiaptomus dorsalis

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Maxillopoda
Subclass
Copepoda
Order
Calanoida
Family
Diaptomidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Each caudal ramus with five setae
  • Third segment from the end of the right antenna with a process which is curved and pointed. The process is shorter than the second segment from the end of the same antenna
  • Lateral spine on the second exopod right fifth leg is located towards the middle of the segment, and is longer than the segement
  • Segment 14 on the right antennae bears a spinous process
