Cercopagis pengoi

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Suborder
Cladocera
Family
Cercopagididae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Body and legs not covered with bivalve carapace
  • Body length ranges from 1 to 2.5 mm, plus an additional 6 to 13 mm tail spine
  • Tail spine has a distinctive loop towards the end of the spine
  • Female brood chamber ends in a point
