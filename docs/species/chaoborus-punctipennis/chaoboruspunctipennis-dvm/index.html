Relative light changes during evening migration

Chaoborus migration

  • Figure illustrates that the rate of migration ascent (velocity) is regulated by the percentage change in light (stimulus). This shows the direct correlation between velocity and the light stimulus. Data is based on sonar traces of Chaoborus larvae migrating in New Hampshire lakes. (Modified from Haney et al., 1990).
