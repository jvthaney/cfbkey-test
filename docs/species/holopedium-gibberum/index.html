Holopedium gibberum

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Suborder
Cladocera
Family
Holopedidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • animal enclosed in large gelatinous mantle (not always visible)
  • 2nd antennae not branched and cylindrical (females)
  • ventral margins of valves with fine spines
  • anal claws with a small basal spine
