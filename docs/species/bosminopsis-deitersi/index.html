Bosminopsis deitersi

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Suborder
Cladocera
Family
Bosminidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Antennules are united at the base and diverge toward the apex
  • One large spine near the base of the postabdominal claw

Distribution

  • Southwestern US, south into South America
