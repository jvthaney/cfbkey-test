Arctodiaptomus floridanus

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Maxillopoda
Subclass
Copepoda
Order
Calanoida
Family
Diaptomidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Each caudal ramus with five setae
  • Third segment from the end of the right antenna with a process which is curved and pointed. The process is shorter than the second segment from the end of the same antenna
  • Lateral spine on the second exopod right 5th leg located near the end of the segment, and is longer than the segment
  • View entire male 5th leg
  • Female caudal ramus with hairs on both the inner and outer margins
