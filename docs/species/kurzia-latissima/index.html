Kurzia latissima

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Suborder
Cladocera
Family
Chydoridae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Postabdominal claw with one basal spine and a secondary tooth in the middle
  • Antennal setae formula 1-1-3 / 0-0-3
  • Postabdomen with 10 to 12 marginal denticles
  • Slight crest on back only, none on head.
