Leptodiaptomus ashlandi

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Maxillopoda
Subclass
Copepoda
Order
Calanoida
Family
Diaptomidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Each caudal ramus with five setae
  • Third segment from the end of the right antenna has a slender process, which is usually strait, blunt and as long or longer than the second segment from the end.
  • Large lateral spine located in proximal third of segment
  • View entire male 5th leg
  • Antenna reaching beyond the distal end of the caudal rami
  • Metasomal wings on female disproportionate
