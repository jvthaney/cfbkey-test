Latona parviremis

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Suborder
Cladocera
Family
Sididae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Antennae with a small lateral expansion on basal joint of dorsal ramus
  • Eye is situated dorsally
  • Antennule with two sections, a basal segment and a seta
  • Postabdomen with 12 to 17 anal teeth
