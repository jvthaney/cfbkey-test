Chaoborus (albipes) flavicans

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Hexapoda
Class
Insecta
Order
Diptera
Family
Chaoboridae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Mandibular fan with less than 12 rays
  • Anal fan with about 22 rays
  • Pre-labral leaf-like appendages about 5 times as long as broad
  • O.A. Johannsen (1934)
