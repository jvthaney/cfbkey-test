Keratella hiemalis

Phylum
Rotifera
Class
Monogononta
Order
Ploima
Family
Brachionidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Postero-lateral facets on the dorsal surface of the lorica closed.
  • Pustules present on lorica surface.
  • The anterior horizontal ridge of anteromedial facet is short relative to the other sides of the facet, giving a distinctly triangular shape.

Ecology

  • Cold stenotherm occuring in winter or in hypolimnion of cold deep lakes.

Additional Notes

  • Can be confused with Keratella testudo.
