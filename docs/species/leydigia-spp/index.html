Leydigia spp.

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Suborder
Cladocera
Family
Chydoridae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Postabdomen large and broad with clusters of long lateral spines in groups
  • Ocellus as large or larger than eye

Taxonomic Notes

  • Worldwide 12 species of Leydigia are accepted as valid, see Kotov (2009) for a key to the genus.

References

(1) KOTOV, A.A. 2009. A revision of Leydigia Kurz, 1875 (Anomopoda, Cladocera, Branchiopoda), and subgeneric differentiation within the genus. Zootaxa. 2082: 1-84.
