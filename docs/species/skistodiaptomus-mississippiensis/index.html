Skistodiaptomus mississippiensis

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Maxillopoda
Subclass
Copepoda
Order
Calanoida
Family
Diaptomidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Each caudal ramus with five setae
  • Third segment from the end of the right antenna without a process or hyaline lamella
  • Right fifth endopod enlarged, extending beyond the length of the first exopod
  • Terminal claw of male 5th leg biangular
  • Spine on right antennule segment 10 and 11 smaller than segment 13
  • Male body length: 0.98 mm; female: 1.08 mm

References

(1) YEATMAN, H.C. 1986. Diaptomus carolinensis, A new species of copepod from North Carolina. J. Tenn. Acad. Sci. 61(4): 85-87.
