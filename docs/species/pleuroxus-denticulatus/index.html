Pleuroxus denticulatus

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Suborder
Cladocera
Family
Chydoridae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Postabdominal claw withtwo basal spines
  • Inferoposteal angle of valves with teeth, number of teeth variable
  • Postanal dorsal margin of postabdomen flat, may have a slight depression distally
  • Cluster of marginal denticles at apex of postabdomen
