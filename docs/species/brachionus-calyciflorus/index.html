Brachionus calyciflorus

Phylum
Rotifera
Class
Monogononta
Order
Ploima
Family
Brachionidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Anteromedian spines have broad base, posterior spines are often present.
  • Lorica is smooth and transparent; appears as one piece.
  • Body length is 180 - 570 µm, body width is 124 - 300 µm, posterolateral spines 0-115 µm, anterolateral dorsal spines 15-95µm, anteromedian dorsal spines 25-200µm.

Ecology

  • Chlorella pyrenoidosa and bacteria are important food items.

Additional Notes

  • One of the most frequently encountered species in Brachionus.
