Rhynochotalona falcata

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Suborder
Cladocera
Family
Chydoridae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Postabdominal claw with one basal spine
  • Postabdomen with two to four large marginal denticles and a long series of lateral denticles
  • Rostrum recurved and extends well past the antennules
