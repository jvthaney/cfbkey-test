Ceriodaphnia lacustris

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Suborder
Cladocera
Family
Daphniidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Rostrum absent
  • Head small and depressed
  • Cervical sinus present
  • Postabdominal claw with fine inconspicuous denticulation
  • Fornices projecting into spinous processes
