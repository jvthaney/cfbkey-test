Moina micrura

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Suborder
Cladocera
Family
Moinidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Anntenules large
  • Distal forked tooth on postabdomen
  • Head with supraocular depression
  • Less than 8 postanal spines
  • No hair on head or valves
  • Specimens small: 0.5 to 1.2 mm
