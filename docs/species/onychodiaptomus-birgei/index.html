Onychodiaptomus birgei

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Maxillopoda
Subclass
Copepoda
Order
Calanoida
Family
Diaptomidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Each caudal ramus with five setae
  • Inner process on second exopod of male left 5th leg distinctly curved
  • Large lateral spine ≥ length of second exopod on male right 5th leg
  • Third segment from the end of the right first antenna has a blunt point at the distal end
  • First segment of the right exopod with a quadrangular appendage
