Keratella cochlearis

Phylum
Rotifera
Class
Monogononta
Order
Ploima
Family
Brachionidae

Additional Notes

  • Keratella cochlearis forms:
  • K.c. cochlearis : Posterior polygons are open.
  • K.c. faluta : Posterior spine stout and wide at base.
  • K.c. hispida : Lorica covered with spinelets, indistinct polygonal facets.
  • K.c. robusta : Posterior polygons are closed.
  • K.c. tecta: Posterior spine absent. (could also be consideredK. tecta)
