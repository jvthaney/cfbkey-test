Eubosmina coregoni

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Suborder
Cladocera
Family
Bosminidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Postabdominal claw with proximal pecten only
  • Sensory bristle near tip of rostrum
  • Mucro absent

Additional Notes

  • Eubosmina as a genus is being eliminated from the literature in favor of Bosmina.
