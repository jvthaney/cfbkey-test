Leptodiaptomus novamexicanus

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Maxillopoda
Subclass
Copepoda
Order
Calanoida
Family
Diaptomidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Each caudal ramus with five setae
  • Third segment from the end of the right antenna has a curved process; the base of the process starts at the middle of the segment.
  • Both terminal processes of the left fifth exopod digitiform and blunt
  • Right fifth endopod longer than the first exopod segment
  • View entire male 5th leg
  • Spine on right antennule segment 11 shorter than that on segment 13
