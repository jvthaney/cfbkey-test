Ergasilus spp.

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Maxillopoda
Subclass
Copepoda
Order
Poecilostomatoida
Family
Ergasilidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Body form is generally similar to a cyclopoid
  • Antennae prehensile, terminating in a claw

Ecology

  • Mature females are parasites found mainly on the gills of fish, while males and copepodids are planktonic.
