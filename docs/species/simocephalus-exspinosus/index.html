Simocephalus exspinosus

Phylum
Arthropoda
Subphylum
Crustacea
Class
Branchiopoda
Order
Cladocera
Family
Daphniidae

Distinguishing Characteristics

  • Postabdominal claw with distinct proximal pecten
  • Ocellus round or rhomboidal
  • Vertex with obtuse or rounded angle

Taxonomic Notes

  • Allozyme studies indicate that Simocephalus exspinosus is a species complex consisting of at least four different species (1).

References

(1) HANN, B.J. 1995. Genetic variation in Simocephalus (Anomopoda: Daphniidae) in North America: patterns and consequences. Hydrobiologia. 307: 9-14.
