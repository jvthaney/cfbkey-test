**General method for preparing whole rotifer specimens for species identification**

1. Transfer the rotifer using a small bore glass pipette to the center of a microscope slide. Minimize the liquid associated with the transfer.
1. Scrape a small of amount of play clay on each of the four corners of a glass coverslip to provide support mounts to keep from crushing the rotifer. Place cover on the drop of water containing the specimen.
1. If necessary orient the specimen for taxonomic examination by gently nudging the coverslip on its clay mounts with a pencil tip or dissecting needle.
